Before you flash

For the original TIDRADIO TD-H3, based on stock 250317. TD-H3 Plus and other models (including HAM) are not validated. Back up your settings and keep stock firmware and a recovery procedure. Unofficial firmware; flash at your own risk.

Good to know.

Do I need to modify the hardware?

No hardware modification is required. The custom artwork and fonts are included in the firmware image. TIDRADIO's flashing software is available on its programming software page.

Can I switch palettes in the radio's menu?

No. Classic Green and Pocket are separate firmware files. Choose the one you prefer when flashing.
